Moves review delivery out of the web process: approve and retry-merge still flip the card to `reviewing`, then enqueue a durable `review_deliveries` row that a worker claims under a per-repo `repo_leases` row (replacing the in-process `withRepoMergeLock`), runs the unchanged merge / pull-request paths, records every base-branch move in a `ref_writes` table, and finishes the card. The run-end integrity check now consults `ref_writes` (waiting for the repo lease to clear before judging an unexplained move) instead of the in-memory `liveBaselines` registry, and the stale-worker reaper finishes dead deliveries, releases their leases and parks the card in Needs Attention. Verified by unit tests plus a new split-process check with two web-only approvals on one repo and a loop overlapping a sibling merge.

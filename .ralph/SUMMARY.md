Adds an acceptance pre-check (spec 31): once the planner's artifacts validate and before the card leaves `planning`, `precheckAcceptance` runs the plan's check-shaped commands against the untouched worktree with the same extraction, allowlist, sandbox wrapping and 30 s timeout as the post-DONE probe. Checks that already exit the way their criterion wants are recorded in a new `acceptance.precheck` event and on the plan row (`plans.precheck_passing`), and the plan is sent back to the planner once through the critic's replan path, sharing the critic's two-revision cap. A plan that still carries such a check ships as written; after DONE its failure is reported in `acceptance.probe` but no longer spends the run's single repair iteration. Checks under a `## Regression` heading in `CRITERIA.md` are skipped by the pre-check and probed after DONE as before; exit 127 (missing binary) and timeouts are treated as unprobed. The planning run now re-checks its abort signal and run-row status after the pre-check and the plan commit so a cancellation cannot resurrect the run. Also included, unrelated to the card: `next.config.ts` sets `turbopack.root` when `node_modules` is symlinked from outside the project so the gate builds in a shared-install worktree.

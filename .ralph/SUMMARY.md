Adds a read-only plan critic stage (spec 30) between planning and the loop: after the planner commits a plan, a `critique` run with its own provider/model/reasoning-level settings reads the card, scoping thread, named spec files and the three plan artifacts, and writes `.ralph/CRITIQUE.md` in the evaluator's verdict format. Approve moves the card to Ready or plan review exactly as planning did; revise re-plans with the critic's feedback in the planner prompt, escalating to human plan review on the third consecutive revise. The critic is on by default for breakdown pieces (`planCriticMode`), with a per-card `planCritic` override and `criticModel`; it gets telemetry, a `critique.jsonl` transcript reachable from `GET /api/runs/[id]`, a `critique.decided` activity event, UI controls, a mock-provider script (`critic-revise-once`), and any edit other than its verdict rejects the verdict to Needs Attention.

Replaces the orchestrator's process-memory guards (`activeLoopCards`, `pendingEvaluations`, `pausedCards`) with database state so several worker processes can share one SQLite database: a `workers` table with boot registration and an upserting heartbeat, `runs.worker_id` on every run, `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` claims for loop starts (`claimLoopRun`), planning/evaluating starts (`claimStage`) and queued evaluations (`claimPendingEvaluation` on a new `cards.evaluation_pending` column), pause as the card's own `paused` status, and a continuous stale reaper (`reapStaleRuns`) that interrupts runs of dead workers, resumes or parks their cards and deletes dead worker rows — boot `recover()` is its first pass plus a scratch sweep that spares live workers' runs. Adds the `workerStaleSeconds` setting (default 120 s, floor 15 s, read at reap time), migration 0018, unit suites for workers/claims/reaper, and extends the two-process split check with cap-contention and SIGKILL-handover scenarios. Evaluator approved with two narrow follow-ups flagged: `cancelCard()` leaves `evaluation_pending` set, and the boot orphan sweep's idle filter can leave a run-less `planning` card stuck after a quick single-process restart.

Moves review delivery (approve / retry-merge merges and pull-request pushes) out of the web process into workers: the web process records a durable `review_deliveries` row and leaves the card in `reviewing`; a worker claims it under a per-repo `repo_leases` row (replacing the in-process `withRepoMergeLock`), runs the unchanged merge/PR code, records moved refs in `ref_writes`, and finishes the card. The run-end integrity check now consults `ref_writes` instead of the removed in-memory `liveBaselines` registry, and the stale-worker reaper fails deliveries of dead workers, releases their leases and parks the card in Needs Attention.

Evaluator verdict: revise — unit tests, tsc and eslint pass, but the new split-process test fails deterministically (writes into a directory `git rm` just deleted) and, once that is patched, is flaky because the run-end ref check can read a moved `main` before the sibling worker's `ref_writes` row lands.

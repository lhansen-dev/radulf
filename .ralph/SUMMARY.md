Adds a read-only plan critic stage (spec 30) between planning and the loop: a new `critique` run kind with its own provider, model, reasoning level, timeout and prompt template reads the card, its scoping thread, the named spec files and the three plan artifacts, then writes `.ralph/CRITIQUE.md` in the evaluator's verdict format. Approve sends the card to Ready or plan review exactly as planning did; revise re-plans with the critic's feedback prefixed in the planner prompt, at most twice, with the third consecutive revise escalating to plan review. The critic may write nothing but its verdict — any other change or commit rejects the verdict to Needs Attention.

The critic is on by default for breakdown pieces via a `planCriticMode` setting (`breakdown`/`always`/`off`) with a per-card `planCritic` override and `criticModel`; runs carry telemetry and a `critique.jsonl` transcript reachable from the runs API, verdicts show as `critique.decided` events on the card, stage diagnosis names the plan critic, and the mock provider gained a critic script plus a `critic-revise-once` scenario covered by the pipeline test.

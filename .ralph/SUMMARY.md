Moves the orchestrator's start/pause/pending-evaluation bookkeeping out of process memory and into SQLite so two worker processes can share one database (spec 25, decisions 2–3): a `workers` table with heartbeats, `runs.worker_id`, a `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` claim for loop runs and queued evaluations, `cards.evaluation_pending`, pause as an immediate card transition, and a continuous stale reaper (also boot recovery's first pass) governed by a new floored `workerStaleSeconds` setting. The two-worker split check (cap contention, SIGKILL handover) and the new claim/reaper/worker suites pass; the verdict is `revise` because the scoping suite regressed on the now-synchronous claim and a JSDoc reintroduces the removed identifier names that the acceptance greps forbid, plus a handful of robustness gaps (heartbeat not an upsert, continuous orphan sweep parking live `reviewing` cards, an unstoppable reaper interval).
